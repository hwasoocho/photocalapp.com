Privacy Policy

PhotoCal: Photo Calendar. Last updated October 5, 2026.

The short version

PhotoCal does not collect, store, or send any of your data. Your photos never leave your device.

Photos

PhotoCal asks for access to your photo library so it can show your photos on a calendar. It reads your photos on your device only. It does not upload, copy, or share them anywhere. To speed things up, it keeps a small list of photo identifiers, dates, sizes and favorite marks on your device; it never contains the photos themselves and is deleted when you delete the app.

When you tap the heart or the trash, on one photo or on photos you selected, PhotoCal marks them as favorites or deletes them, only after you tap to do so. These changes are made by your phone's own photo library, so they also show in the Photos app. Deleted photos go to Recently Deleted, where you can recover them for 30 days.

Calendar

If you allow calendar access, PhotoCal shows the events on your phone's calendar next to that day's photos. Day notes are added with your phone's own event editor and saved in your Calendar app, so they sync wherever your calendar already syncs. PhotoCal reads your calendar on your device only and sends nothing anywhere.

No accounts, no tracking

PhotoCal has no sign-up, no servers, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind.

Purchases

The optional one-time unlock is bought through the App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google handle the payment under their own privacy policies. PhotoCal only learns whether the unlock was purchased.

Sharing

If you share a photo, PhotoCal hands it to your phone's own share sheet. Where it goes is up to you.

Children

PhotoCal collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: support@photocalapp.com.
