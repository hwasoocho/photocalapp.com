Privacy Policy

PhotoCal: Photo Calendar. Last updated October 4, 2026.

The short version

PhotoCal does not collect, store, or send any of your data. Your photos never leave your device.

Photos

PhotoCal asks for access to your photo library so it can show your photos on a calendar. It reads your photos on your device only. It does not upload, copy, or share them anywhere. To speed things up, it keeps a small list of photo identifiers and dates on your device; it never contains the photos themselves and is deleted when you delete the app.

No accounts, no tracking

PhotoCal has no sign-up, no servers, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind.

Purchases

The optional one-time unlock is bought through the App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google handle the payment under their own privacy policies. PhotoCal only learns whether the unlock was purchased.

Sharing

If you share a photo, PhotoCal hands it to your phone's own share sheet. Where it goes is up to you.

Children

PhotoCal collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: hscho817@gmail.com.
