Common questions

I don't see my photos. PhotoCal needs access to your photo library. On iPhone open Settings, then PhotoCal, then Photos, and choose Full Access. On Android open Settings, then Apps, then PhotoCal, then Permissions.

Some dates are blurred. The last 30 days are free. Unlock every day with the one-time purchase to see older dates.

I bought the unlock on another phone. Tap a blurred date, then Restore Purchase. Use the same Apple ID or Google account you bought it with.

Do my photos get uploaded? No. Everything stays on your device. See the Privacy Policy.
